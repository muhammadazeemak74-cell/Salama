# Your own photographs

Anything you drop in here wins over the stock fetch.

Name the file after the slot it should fill and the build picks it up:

    photos/gal-01.jpg          ->  public/images/gal-01.webp
    photos/hero.png            ->  public/images/hero.webp
    photos/svc-keratin.webp    ->  public/images/svc-keratin.webp

`.jpg`, `.jpeg`, `.png`, `.webp`, `.avif` and `.tif` all work. The build crops
each one to that slot's exact dimensions (see IMAGE-BRIEF.md) and converts it —
you do not need to resize anything first, though a photo larger than the target
will always look better than one smaller.

No colour grade is applied to these. The grade exists to make unrelated stock
photographs look like a single shoot; your own work does not need it, and
restyling a client's before/after would misrepresent the result.

**This is where the real gallery goes.** The ten `gal-*` slots currently show
stock photography and must be replaced with genuine client work before any paid
traffic is sent to the site.

Get written permission from any client whose hair appears here.
